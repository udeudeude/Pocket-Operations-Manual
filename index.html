Select a sound or tap a pad.

Guided lessons validate the actions you take on the virtual instrument. Use the device itself to practice.

Play and sequence

Physical chords: hold one function key while pressing a numbered pad. One-handed: tap SOUND or PATTERN then a pad; double-tap FX then a pad. Tap FX once to change knob bank. Drag the SVG knobs vertically.

Slots 1–15 use synthetic practice voices until you make microphone recordings. Slot 16 plays synthesized Tonic-style drums.

No chain

In a chain, patterns may repeat and the entire chain loops. Step multipliers and recorded knob movements are saved per pattern.

Keyboard: 1–8 and Q–I trigger pads 1–16; Space plays/stops; W toggles WRITE.

Recording studio

Record up to 8 seconds into slots 1–15. Microphone captures are retained in this browser when IndexedDB is available; recorded samples receive voice and FX processing during playback.

Microphone permission is requested only when recording.

Save / restore

Pattern JSON includes step notes, chains, voice/effect automation, and parameter locks. It does not include recorded audio, and is not a hardware-compatible PO-35 data-transfer backup.
